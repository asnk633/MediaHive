# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Workspace managers, operators, and field agents coordinating media, tasks, calendar events, and attendance. They operate in multi-tenant environments (including high-integrity or security-focused enclaves) requiring high reliability and offline utility.

## Product Purpose
To provide a secure, high-performance, and mobile-first platform for managing and syncing media, tasks, events, and attendance securely across isolated workspace tenants.

## Brand Personality
Professional, institutional, and minimal. The interface priorities are grayscale-first layouts, strict high contrast (supporting clear legibility), and clean, distraction-free visual structure.

## Anti-references
- SaaS/AI monoculture templates (cream/sand/beige default backgrounds, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section, numbered section kickers `01 / 02 / 03`).
- Dribbble-style glassmorphic aura glows, neon gradients, and side-stripe border accents.
- Ambiguous touch targets and low-contrast metadata text.

## Design Principles
1. **Contrast over Color**: Rely on typography weights, scales, and clean whitespace partitions to convey visual hierarchy rather than introducing random color tokens.
2. **Deterministic Structural Layout**: Adhere strictly to the 8pt spacing grid and frozen border-radius constraints (4px/8px/12px) to present an orderly, trustworthy utility.
3. **Air-Gap & Mobile Resilience**: Prioritize low-density and highly visible actions for mobile screens, ensuring robust interaction states (focus indicator, min 44px tap target) and support for offline check-ins.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict contrast checks (>=4.5:1 for body and placeholders).
- Focus-visible focus rings for keyboard navigation.
- Minimum 44px touch targets for touch devices.
- Every layout and interface state must remain fully functional and readable under grayscale/monochrome review.
