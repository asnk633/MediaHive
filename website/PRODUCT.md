# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Institutions with multi-tier organization hierarchies, media managers, and system administrators looking for a premium media management, scheduling, and accountability platform.

## Product Purpose
A marketing landing page and interactive showcase for the MediaHive platform. Its goal is to visually wow visitors, guiding them through a cinematic 3D WebGL tour of the application and demonstrating its desktop, mobile, and web compatibility.

## Brand Personality
Premium, Cinematic, Immersive. It evokes a feeling of high-end craftsmanship, seamless technology coordination, and cinematic responsiveness, using dark themes, interactive WebGL, and custom Web Audio synthesis.

## Anti-references
- Generic flat SaaS template designs
- Bland or gray layouts without visual depth
- Tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section (kicker trope)
- Glassmorphic card grids repeated endlessly
- Side-stripe borders as colored accents on cards

## Design Principles
- **Cinematic Immersion**: Use scroll-driven 3D renders, WebGL animations, and smooth transitions to tell a unified story.
- **Authentic Feedback**: Integrate high-fidelity client-side audio synthesis and micro-interactions that respond dynamically to user input.
- **Structured Rhythm**: Organize information using structured bento-box layouts and variable spacing, avoiding boring, identical card grids.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict support for `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` to disable heavy WebGL rotations, camera sweeps, and motion.
- High contrast text on dark backgrounds (>= 4.5:1 ratio).
- Mute option/toggle for ambient synthesizer audio.
- Clear heading structure and semantic HTML for screen readers.
